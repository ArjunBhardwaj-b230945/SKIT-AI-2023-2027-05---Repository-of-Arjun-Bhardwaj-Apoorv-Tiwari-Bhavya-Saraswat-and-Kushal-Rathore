Example conversation preview

Preview — not interactive
You
Can I claim deduction for my health insurance premium?
TaxSathi AI
Yes! Under Section 80D, you can claim up to ₹25,000 for health insurance premiums paid for yourself, your spouse, and dependent children. If your parents are also covered, you can claim an additional ₹25,000 (₹50,000 if they are senior citizens).
You
What documents do I need?
TaxSathi AI
You'll need the premium payment receipt from your insurer. The actual deduction is self-declared in your ITR — no document needs to be submitted to the department.
